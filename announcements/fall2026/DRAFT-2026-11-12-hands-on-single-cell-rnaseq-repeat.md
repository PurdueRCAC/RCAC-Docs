### Who Should Attend

Researchers who have, or plan to generate, single-cell RNA-seq data and want to analyze it themselves in R on RCAC clusters, from raw data to annotated cell types and differential expression.

### What You’ll Learn

* How single-cell data goes from raw reads to a count matrix, and how to judge its quality
* Normalization, feature selection, dimensionality reduction, and clustering with Seurat
* Cell type annotation, multi-sample integration, and differential expression across conditions

### By the End of the Workshop, You’ll

* Run a complete single-cell RNA-seq analysis in RStudio on an RCAC cluster
* Cluster and annotate cells and integrate multiple samples into one analysis
* Test for differential expression between conditions and produce figures from your own data

### Level

**Intermediate**. Basic Linux/command-line and R skills (installing packages, reading data, making plots) are expected; no prior single-cell experience required.

**Required pre-work:** Complete the lesson’s [Summary and Setup](https://rcac-bioinformatics.github.io/singlecell-rnaseq/) page before the workshop. SSH access to the cluster and data staging must be done before the session starts.
