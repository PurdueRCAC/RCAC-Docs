### Who Should Attend

Biologists curious about using AI assistants and large language models in everyday research work: writing and debugging code, reading documentation, and drafting analyses.

### What You’ll Learn

* Where AI tools genuinely help in a bioinformatics workflow, and where they mislead
* How to use an AI assistant to write, explain, and debug analysis code
* How to check AI-generated output against your data, and what not to share with a model

### By the End of the Session, You’ll

* Use an AI assistant to draft and debug a script for a real task
* Evaluate an AI answer critically before acting on it
* Decide which parts of your work are appropriate for AI assistance

### Level

**Basic**. No programming experience required.
