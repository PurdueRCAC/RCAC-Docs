### Who Should Attend

Faculty, postdocs, and graduate students in the life sciences who are new to Purdue or new to research computing and want a clear path from “I have an account” to running a first analysis on RCAC systems.

### What You’ll Learn

* What RCAC offers biologists: clusters, storage, software, web portals, and where to get help
* How to log in, move data, find software, and submit a first job
* How to organize a project and choose the right resources so your first analysis is also a reproducible one

### By the End of the Workshop, You’ll

* Log in to an RCAC cluster and find your way around its storage and software
* Submit and monitor a basic job and open interactive tools through the web portal
* Plan where the data, software, and analyses for a new project will live

### Level

**Basic**. No prior computing experience required.
