### Who Should Attend

Biologists and bioinformaticians at any career stage who run, or plan to run, genomics analyses on RCAC clusters and want that work to be repeatable by their future selves, their lab, and their reviewers.

### What You’ll Learn

* What “reproducible” means in practice for a genomics project: software environments, versions, inputs, and provenance
* Where RCAC clusters, storage tiers, and software fit into a reproducible analysis
* The Fall 2026 roadmap: how the data management, Nextflow, nf-core, R/Bioconductor, and AI sessions build on one another

### By the End of the Session, You’ll

* Describe the pieces a reproducible genomics analysis needs on a shared cluster
* Identify which RCAC resources your project should use for compute, active data, shared data, and archive
* Choose the sessions this season that match your project’s next step

### Level

**Basic**. No prior HPC or bioinformatics experience required.
