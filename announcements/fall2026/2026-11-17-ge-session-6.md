### Who Should Attend

Researchers who prefer a web browser to a terminal and want to launch nf-core pipelines on RCAC clusters through the Open OnDemand portal.

### What You’ll Learn

* How to reach RCAC clusters through the Open OnDemand web portal
* How to set up inputs and parameters and launch an nf-core pipeline from the portal
* How to follow a run’s progress and find its results and reports from the browser

### By the End of the Session, You’ll

* Launch an nf-core pipeline from Open OnDemand on an RCAC cluster
* Track a running pipeline and retrieve its outputs and reports
* Decide when the portal is enough and when to move to the command line

### Level

**Basic**. No command-line experience required.
