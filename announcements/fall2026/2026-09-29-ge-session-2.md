### Who Should Attend

Researchers who generate or receive sequencing data and need to store, move, share, and describe it responsibly, including anyone writing a data management plan for a proposal.

### What You’ll Learn

* Which RCAC storage service fits each stage of a project: scratch for active work, Depot for shared lab data, Fortress for long-term archive
* How to move large datasets reliably with Globus, between clusters and to and from collaborators and sequencing centers
* What reviewers look for in a data management plan, with ready-to-adapt DMP text that describes RCAC storage

### By the End of the Session, You’ll

* Place your project’s data in the right storage service and explain why
* Transfer large datasets with Globus instead of fragile ad hoc copies
* Draft the storage and sharing sections of a data management plan using RCAC services

### Level

**Basic**. Suitable for users at all experience levels.
