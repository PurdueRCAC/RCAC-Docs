### Who Should Attend

Researchers and students who want to organize their bioinformatics projects to improve reproducibility, clarity, and long-term maintainability.

### What You’ll Learn

* Recommended folder structures for genomics projects
* Naming conventions for scripts, data, and results
* Strategies for keeping analyses understandable months or years later

### By the End of the Session, You’ll

* Have a practical project organization template
* Understand how good organization supports reproducibility
* Be able to restructure existing projects more effectively
