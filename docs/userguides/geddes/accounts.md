# Accounts

## Obtaining access to Geddes

Access to the Geddes Composable Platform is handled via the RCAC web portal. When access is purchased, a Rancher project with your research group's name will be created and managers will be able to click to give users access, similar to how access is managed for community clusters.

To obtain access, please [contact support](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/help) with your request.
