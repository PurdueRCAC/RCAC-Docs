# Genomics Exchange, Fall 2026

The Fall 2026 season of the **Genomics Exchange** runs eight online sessions on Tuesdays, 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM Eastern, from September 15 through December 8, 2026. Session dates are not evenly spaced this season, so check the schedule below. Each session lasts 60 minutes on Microsoft Teams and pairs a short lecture with a hands-on walkthrough.

Sessions in September and October are at 11:00 AM **EDT**; sessions in November and December are at 11:00 AM **EST** (US daylight time ends Sunday, November 1, 2026). The local Purdue time is 11:00 AM throughout.

!!! note "Registration"
    Register for each session through its Microsoft Teams link below. The Teams join link and email reminders go to registrants; you must register for each session you want to attend.
