## Get help

!!! tip "Did you know?"
    RCAC offers live help with our [Coffee Hour Consultations](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/coffee) and [Anvil Support Hour](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/anvil/anvil-support-hour). Reserve one of the available time slots that works best for you! For more in-depth problems or group training, One-on-One Consultations are also available. [Contact us](mailto:rcac-help@purdue.edu) to schedule a one-on-one consultation for you or your lab.

- [Downloads](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/downloads) maintains and/or recommends software available for download to personal and Purdue campus computers.

- [Current and Planned Maintenance and Outages](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/news/outages) are listed here.

### Email

**How to send**

- For **Anvil-specific** requests, please follow the instructions below:

    !!! question "How to submit a request related to Anvil?"
        We have different ticketing systems for general Anvil users and NAIRR users.
        
        === "Anvil Users"
            Please submit a ticket through the **ACCESS Help Desk** below and select the appropriate Anvil resource to have it properly routed.

            [Submit a ticket to ACCESS Help Desk](https://support.access-ci.org/help-ticket){ .md-button }

        === "NAIRR Users"
            Please submit a ticket through the **NAIRR ticket portal** below. 

            [Submit a ticket through NAIRR ticket portal](https://nairrpilot.org/open-support-request){ .md-button }

- For **Purdue-related** requests, email us at [rcac-help@purdue.edu](mailto:rcac-help@purdue.edu).

**To help us respond, please include**

- Cluster or resource name
- Copy and paste or screenshot of any error messages
- Job IDs (if applicable)

### In-Person

- [Coffee Hour Consultations](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/coffee) offer a regular place to drop in for quick questions and help. No appointment needed!
- [Anvil Support Hour](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/anvil/anvil-support-hour) offers the opportunity to discuss Anvil related questions with the Anvil support team. NAIRR users are also welcome to register!
- [Training Sessions](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/news/events) are offered in person regularly in the Envision Center on a variety of topics.
- [One-on-One Consultations](mailto:rcac-help@purdue.edu) are available. Just email us to schedule a one-on-one consultation for you or your lab.
- [Schedule a tour](https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/about/tour) — group tours are available for our Data Center and Envision Center facilities!
